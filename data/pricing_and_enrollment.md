## 2. Discounts and Special Promotional Packages
- **Quarterly Upfront Payment (Pago Trimestral):** 10% discount when paying 3 consecutive modules in advance.
- **Full Level Bundle (Pago por Nivel Completo - e.g., Full B1):** 15% discount when enrolling and paying for the complete level in a single transaction.

## 3. Accepted Payment Methods in Colombia
We offer secure electronic and physical payment channels:
1. **PSE (Pagos Seguros en Línea):** Instant debit from any Colombian bank account (Bancolombia, Davivienda, BBVA, Banco de Bogotá, Nequi, Daviplata).
2. **Credit and Debit Cards:** Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Diners Club (available online and at physical campus dataphones with up to 12 installments).
3. **Direct Bank Transfer / Cash Deposit:**
   - **Bancolombia:** Savings Account (Cuenta de Ahorros) # 108-987654-21, Convenio # 78420.
   - **Davivienda:** Checking Account (Cuenta Corriente) # 045-123456-78.
   - *Receipt/Voucher must be uploaded to the student portal or emailed to billing@colombialanguageacademy.edu.co.*
4. **Campus Front Desk:** Cash and dataphone accepted at Bogotá (Calle 72 & Usaquén) and Medellín (El Poblado & Laureles) campuses.
5. **Educational Financing:** Zero-interest installments via Addi and Sistecredito directly at checkout.

## 4. Enrollment Calendar and Deadlines
- **Monthly Intakes:** New cohorts open **twice a month**:
  - Cohort A: First Monday of every month.
  - Cohort B: Third Monday of every month.
- **Enrollment Deadline:** Registrations and payment confirmations close **3 business days prior** to the cohort start date at 06:00 PM (COT).
- **Late Registration:** Subject to seat availability and a late administrative fee of $30,000 COP.

---

## 5. Cancellation, Refund, and Deferral (Congelación) Policies
- **Prior to Course Start (≥ 5 business days):** 100% refund of tuition fees (minus a 5% banking transaction fee). Registration fee is non-refundable.
- **Prior to Course Start (< 5 business days):** 80% refund or 100% credit toward future courses.
- **After Course Starts (Before the 2nd class session):** 70% credit voucher towards any academy program valid for 12 months. No cash refunds.
- **After the 2nd class session:** Strictly no refunds or credits for unattended classes.
- **Course Deferral / Freezing (Congelación de Matrícula):** Students facing medical emergencies, job relocation, or academic conflicts may officially freeze their course for up to **6 months** without penalty by submitting a written request to academic@colombialanguageacademy.edu.co at least 48 hours in advance.
