# Colombia Language Academy - Courses and Levels

## 1. Overview and Mission
Colombia Language Academy (Academia de Idiomas Colombia) is a premier language training institution with physical campuses in Bogotá and Medellín, as well as an interactive live online platform serving students across Colombia and Latin America. We offer communicative, immersion-based language learning aligned with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR).

---

## 2. Languages Offered
We provide comprehensive language training in the following 6 languages:
1. **English (Inglés):** General English, Business English, and Kids & Teens English.
2. **French (Francés):** General French and Academic French.
3. **German (Alemán):** General German and Technical/Work Visa German.
4. **Portuguese (Portugués):** Brazilian Portuguese for professionals and travelers.
5. **Italian (Italiano):** General Italian and Cultural Studies.
6. **Spanish for Foreigners (Español para Extranjeros):** Latin American Spanish immersion.

---

## 3. CEFR Level Structure and Progression
Our curricula strictly follow the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), dividing each major level into manageable modular courses:

| CEFR Level | Academy Modules | Total Guided Hours | Description & Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A1 - Beginner** | A1.1, A1.2 | 80 Hours | Understand basic phrases, introduce oneself, ask simple questions. |
| **A2 - Elementary** | A2.1, A2.2, A2.3 | 120 Hours | Communicate routine tasks, describe background and immediate environment. |
| **B1 - Intermediate** | B1.1, B1.2, B1.3 | 120 Hours | Deal with most travel situations, produce simple connected text on topics of interest. |
| **B2 - Upper Intermediate** | B2.1, B2.2, B2.3 | 120 Hours | Understand complex texts, interact with native speakers fluently and spontaneously. |
| **C1 - Advanced** | C1.1, C1.2, C1.3 | 120 Hours | Flexible and effective language use for social, academic, and professional purposes. |

*Note: Each module represents 40 instructional class hours plus autonomous digital platform practice.*

## 4. Learning Modalities

### A. Live Online Virtual Classes (Modalidad Virtual en Vivo)
- Conducted via high-definition interactive video classrooms (Zoom / Google Meet integrated).
- Small group size: 6 to 12 students per cohort.
- 24/7 access to our Learning Management System (LMS) with interactive digital workbooks, audio labs, and AI speaking practice.
- Recorded sessions available for review up to 30 days after class.

### B. In-Person Campus Classes (Modalidad Presencial)
- Modern smart classrooms equipped with multimedia interactive boards and conversation lounges.
- Campus Locations:
  - **Bogotá - Financial District:** Calle 72 # 10-34, Chapinero.
  - **Bogotá - Usaquén:** Carrera 7 # 120-20, Usaquén.
  - **Medellín - El Poblado:** Carrera 43A # 5A-113, El Poblado.
  - **Medellín - Laureles:** Circular 73 # 39B-05, Laureles.

## 5. Schedules and Study Tracks

### Track 1: Standard Track (Intensidad Regular)
- **Schedule:** Monday to Thursday (2 hours per day, 8 hours/week).
- **Time Slots:**
  - Morning: 07:00 AM - 09:00 AM
  - Midday: 12:00 PM - 02:00 PM
  - Evening: 06:30 PM - 08:30 PM
- **Pace:** 1 Module (40 hours) completed every 5 weeks.

### Track 2: Super Intensive Track (Intensivo Diario)
- **Schedule:** Monday to Friday (3 hours per day, 15 hours/week).
- **Time Slots:**
  - Morning: 08:00 AM - 11:00 AM
  - Evening: 06:00 PM - 09:00 PM
- **Pace:** 1 Module (40 hours) completed every 2.5 to 3 weeks.

### Track 3: Saturday Intensive Track (Sábados Intensivos)
- **Schedule:** Every Saturday (5 hours per session).
- **Time Slots:**
  - Saturday Morning: 08:00 AM - 01:00 PM
  - Saturday Afternoon: 01:30 PM - 06:30 PM
- **Pace:** 1 Module completed every 8 weeks (20 hours/month).

### Track 4: Private 1-on-1 Customized Coaching (Clases Personalizadas)
- Tailored schedule according to student availability (Monday to Saturday, 06:00 AM to 09:00 PM).
- Customized syllabus: Medical English, Legal French, Business Pitching, or Accent Reduction.
