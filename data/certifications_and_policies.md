# Colombia Language Academy - Certifications, Academic Policies & Support Escalation

## 1. Official Certifications & Examination Preparation

### A. Academy Diplomas and Certificates
- **Certificate of Occupational Competence (Certificado de Aptitud Ocupacional):** Issued upon completion of full B2 or C1 tracks, accredited under Colombian Ministry of Education guidelines (Resolución Secretaría de Educación de Bogotá / Medellín).
- **Module Completion Verification (Constancia de Aprobación):** Downloadable digitally with QR verification code upon successfully passing each 40-hour module.

### B. International Certification Prep Courses
We offer dedicated test preparation cohorts and official simulated mock exams for:
- **English:** IELTS (Academic & General Training), TOEFL iBT, Cambridge English Qualifications (B2 First - FCE, C1 Advanced - CAE).
- **French:** DELF (A1-B2) and DALF (C1).
- **German:** Goethe-Zertifikat (A1-C1) and TestDaF.
- **Portuguese:** CELPE-Bras.
- **Spanish:** DELE and SIELE certifications.
- *Prep courses include 3 full computer-delivered mock tests with personalized feedback on Speaking and Writing.*

## 2. Academic Requirements and Grading System

### A. Grading Scale (Colombian Standard 0.0 to 5.0)
- **Passing Grade:** Minimum **3.5 / 5.0** cumulative score.
- **Grade Breakdown:**
  - **30% Continuous Assessment:** Weekly quizzes, active participation in class, homework assignments.
  - **30% Midterm Evaluation:** Mid-module oral interview and listening comprehension test.
  - **40% Final Exam:** Comprehensive evaluation covering Reading, Writing, Listening, and 1-on-1 Oral Presentation.

### B. Attendance Policy (Asistencia Obligatoria)
- **Minimum Attendance:** Students must attend at least **80% of scheduled class hours** to be eligible for the final evaluation.
- **Absences and Justifications:** Justified medical or work leaves (with official supporting document) must be submitted within 3 days to avoid academic penalty.
- **Make-up Sessions:** Students may attend up to **2 group make-up sessions** per module at no extra charge. Virtual students have full access to class video recordings.

### C. Remedial Evaluations (Habilitación) and Retakes
- If a student's final grade is between **3.0 and 3.4**, they are entitled to take a **Remedial Exam (Examen de Habilitación)** for $60,000 COP. A score of 3.5 or higher updates the module status to Passed.
- If a student scores below **3.0**, they must repeat the module. Repeat students receive an automatic **30% discount** on the repeating module's tuition.
